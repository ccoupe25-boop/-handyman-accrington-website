Rental maintenance often involves several small jobs at once. Handyman Multi-Trade Solutions helps landlords and property professionals in Accrington and nearby areas with repairs, installations and refreshes, including work between tenancies.

Repairs between tenancies

Send a room-by-room list of the work needed before the property is ready for its next tenants. This can include damaged finishes, general repairs and practical installations.

Bathroom maintenance

Bath resealing, shower screen fitting, replacement fittings and bathroom refreshes. Include photos of the problem and any replacement products already selected.

Carpentry and finishing

Door and trim repairs, shelving installation, boxing-in and other practical timber work around a rental property.

Decorating and general upkeep

Patch repairs, decorating, maintenance and exterior work can be discussed together. Clear photos help identify what is needed and what needs closer inspection.

Make the job list easy to quote

Send your property’s postcode, a list of repairs and clear photos of each item. Mention whether the property is occupied, any access arrangements, and the date you hope to have the work completed. Availability and the scope can then be discussed before a booking is agreed.

For several jobs, group photos by room and explain which items are most urgent. If the work depends on a replacement screen, tap or other fitting, include the product details and whether it has arrived.
