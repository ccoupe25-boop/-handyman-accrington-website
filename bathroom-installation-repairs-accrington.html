Bathroom work, from a small repair to a full refit

Handyman Multi-Trade Solutions carries out bathroom installations, refurbishment and repairs for homeowners and landlords in Accrington and nearby areas. Whether you are replacing a tired bathroom or sorting out one problem, send Colin the details to discuss the work.

Full bathroom installations

Removal of the existing bathroom and installation of replacement fittings, with the work discussed around your room, chosen products and required finish.

Bathroom refurbishment

Replacement fittings, bathroom wall panels and finishing work can refresh an existing space. Send photos of the current bathroom and explain which parts you want to keep.

Shower screen fitting

Fitting and replacement of bath and shower screens. Include a photo of the bath or shower area, the screen details and whether the screen has already been bought.

Bath resealing and repairs

Removal of old silicone, cleaning and preparation, followed by fresh sealing. Tap repairs, replacement fittings and other bathroom maintenance can also be discussed.

What to send for a bathroom quote

Include your postcode, clear photos, a short description of the job and any measurements you have. If you have selected a suite, screen or wall panels, send the product details too. Colin can then discuss the scope and whether a visit is needed before quoting.

For a rental property, mention any access arrangements and whether the work is being planned between tenancies.
